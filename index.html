Six days. Seven nights.
One sacred circuit.

Braj is not a destination — it is the ground Krishna walked as a child. This yatra moves through five towns exactly as the leela unfolded: birth in Mathura, love in Vrindavan, the lifted hill at Govardhan, Radha's home at Barsana, and Krishna's own village at Nandgaon and Gokul.
